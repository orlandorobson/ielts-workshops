## Shared learner instructions

**WRITING TASK 2**

You should spend about 40 minutes on this task.

Write at least 250 words.

Support your answer with reasons and relevant examples, drawing on what you know or have experienced. Write a connected essay in full sentences.

These shared instructions accompany every complete writing task. Type labels and intended-use notes are teacher metadata and should not appear in an unaided examination-style prompt.

## Core workshop questions

### WT2-W01 — Agreement: public-library spending

Some people believe that public libraries should spend more money on printed books than on digital resources.

To what extent do you agree or disagree?

### WT2-W02 — Both views and opinion: public parks

Some people think public parks should mainly provide quiet natural spaces, while others think they should include more sports facilities.

Discuss both views and give your own opinion.

### WT2-W03 — Advantages and disadvantages: cashless payments

In some countries, people are increasingly using cards and mobile phones instead of cash to pay for goods and services.

What are the advantages and disadvantages of this development?

### WT2-W04 — Outweigh judgement: free museum entry

Some museums allow visitors to enter free of charge rather than charging an admission fee.

Do the advantages of this approach outweigh the disadvantages?

### WT2-W05 — Problems and solutions: tourism in small communities

Large numbers of tourists visit some small towns and villages.

What problems can this cause for local residents, and what measures could be taken to address these problems?

### WT2-W06 — Mixed reasons and evaluation: adult online courses

More adults are choosing to study courses online instead of attending classes in person.

Why is this happening? Is this a positive or negative development?

## Additional demand variants

### WT2-W07 — Causes and solutions: city noise

Many people living in cities experience high levels of noise in their daily lives.

What are the main causes of this problem, and what could be done to reduce it?

### WT2-W08 — Causes and effects: shared family meals

In many countries, family members are eating meals together less often than in the past.

Why is this happening, and what effects can it have on family life?

### WT2-W09 — Positive or negative development: online performances

An increasing number of people choose to watch concerts and plays online rather than attend performances in person.

Is this a positive or negative development?

## Use and preservation rules

- Use WT2-W01–WT2-W06 as the core teaching bank. WT2-W07–WT2-W09 provide additional demand variants; they are not extra compulsory lessons.
- WT2-W06 is the recommended continuing learner task through planning, drafting and revision.
- Reserve an untaught question before allocating unseen transfer. Once a model or worked plan has been taught, that question is no longer unseen.
