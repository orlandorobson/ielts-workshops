When I say digital marketing, what comes into your head? Instagram? Ads? Followers? [chuckling] Yeah, usually. Actually, I had a student last year who came in on the first day and told me very proudly that he'd already got nearly 12,000 followers, and I thought, "Okay, that's pretty good." But 12,000 what? 12,000 people who might actually buy something from you? 12,000 people who live on the other side of the world? 

12,000 people who watched one funny video six months ago and never came back? 

Those are completely different things, and this is the bit about marketing that I actually find fascinating. We get distracted by the numbers, 10,000 followers, 100,000 views, a million impressions, but behind every one of those numbers is a person. Somebody wants something. Somebody needs something. Somebody has a problem. 

And if you don't know who that person is, 

what exactly are you marketing? 

So that's where we're going to start. Not with Instagram, not with TikTok, not with Google. With people.
