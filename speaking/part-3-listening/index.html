What can you learn from this speaker?

Five things you can learn from this speaker

  1. Start with a simple answer

    He says: "Cars, I think. Expensive cars."

    He gives an answer immediately, then adds more information.

    Try: "I think..." / "Yes, probably." / "Not really."

  2. Use an example

    He says: "My father, for example, he don't buy expensive clothes."

    He uses someone he knows to help explain his idea.

    Try: "For example..." / "Like..." / "In my family..."

  3. Show another side

    He says: "But if somebody have money, it's okay."

    He shows that there can be more than one way to think about something.

    Try: "But sometimes..." / "But not everybody..." / "On the other hand..."

  4. Check the question

    He says: "Control? You mean like stop some advertising?"

    He checks what the examiner means before answering.

    Try: "Do you mean...?" / "You mean...?"

  5. Give a reason

    He says: "Because you see something many times and then you want to try."

    He explains an idea by showing how one thing leads to another.

    Try: "Because..." / "So..." / "And then..."
