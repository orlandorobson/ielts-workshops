Speaking Part 3 — Discussion

In the IELTS test, Part 3 is a conversation with the examiner. You discuss different ideas, explain your opinions and give reasons.

In this practice, you will listen to recorded questions and answer them in your own words.

Try to explain your ideas clearly. You can stop recording when you finish speaking.

Your answers will be saved for feedback.

Practise any number of questions in one set. Submit Part 3 separately from Parts 1 and 2. Each answer stops automatically after 90 seconds.
