How to complete your writing

  1. Choose a question by clicking on it in the question menu.
  2. You can write your answer directly in the box below, or write it in a document on your computer and then copy and paste your answer into the box.
  3. Start a 40-minute timer on your phone as soon as you start writing. Try to finish within 40 minutes. Don't worry if you go over the time — this will help you develop a better sense of the time available in the IELTS test.
  4. Write at least 250 words.
